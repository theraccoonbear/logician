Logician — Where math meets the mystic.

Two players duel across a hex board of four mystical terrains, building Pools, Pyramids, and Towers — then fortifying them behind Fortresses. Each turn, craft a spell by combining a Logic card (AND, OR, NOT…) with an Effect card (Upgrade, Downgrade, Destroy…) and aim it through one of three face-up Tarot cards that determine what gets hit. The 22 Major Arcana blow the rules wide open — Death wipes the board clean, The Fool flips your opponent's plan, The Devil forces a blind strike with no effect card at all.

Structures level up or get crushed down to nothing. Fortresses protect what's inside — but lock you out of upgrading them too. First to 40 points wins. Every decision is a calculus of risk, timing, and knowing when to shield and when to strike.
